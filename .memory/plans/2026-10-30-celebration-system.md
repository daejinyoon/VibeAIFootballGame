# Plan: Goal Celebrations

## Goal
Campaign goals play one of five three-second team celebrations: SIUUU, 문워크, L을 가져가, 슬릭백, or coordinated RKO. The user chooses a ceremony or random mode in their profile; random mode selects once per match. The scoring team's three field players perform together while match time continues. Overtime results appear after the ceremony; PvP remains unchanged.
