# Plan: Goal Celebration Cinematic Camera

## Goal
Make five-second goal ceremonies easy to watch: show every participant close-up without overlap, show the scoring team's front, and keep players in their own halves until kickoff is played.

- Preserve the five existing ceremony IDs, selected/random ceremony profile behavior, RKO role assignment, and the match clock continuing during celebrations.
- The current ceremony timeline and goal-pause are three seconds; this plan changes both to five seconds.
- The current camera follows the ball; the celebration's `participantIds` and `team` determine its close-up frame and front-facing side.
