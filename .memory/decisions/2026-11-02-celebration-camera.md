# Decisions: Goal Celebration Cinematic Camera

- Date: 2026-11-02
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Celebration camera focus
- **Chosen**: During a goal celebration, smoothly move the camera to frame all celebration participants, zoom in with a lower front-facing angle, then return to the normal tracking camera when the celebration ends.
- **Rationale**: The current camera follows the ball and leaves the celebration group too small to inspect.

## D02. Team-facing camera side
- **Chosen**: Select the approach side from the scoring team so the celebrating players' fronts are visible for either attacking direction.
- **Rationale**: Home and away teams face opposite directions; one fixed camera side would show one team's backs.

## D03. Celebration duration and match timing
- **Chosen**: Make the full ceremony five seconds, including choreography and close camera; begin kickoff after those five seconds. The match clock continues to advance during the ceremony, as already decided. Audio clips play their supplied duration and may end before the ceremony.
- **Rationale**: The longer close-up should show the full motion sequence without stopping the match clock.

## D04. Non-RKO participant formation
- **Chosen**: Arrange general dance participants in a wider, shallow arc across the camera frame so they remain side by side and visible.
- **Rationale**: The current 24-field-unit line can overlap visually at close camera range.

## D05. RKO participant formation
- **Chosen**: Keep the RKO target in the middle, with the striker and finisher separated on the two sides of the camera frame. Preserve their existing roles and action order.
- **Rationale**: This keeps all three actions visible while preserving the agreed RKO choreography.

## D06. Kickoff half-line rule
- **Chosen**: While kickoff is locked, every player except the kickoff taker stays in their own half. Release this restriction once the kickoff ball has been played and moves clear; normal play can then cross the halfway line.
- **Rationale**: Prevent players from running into the opponent's half before the kickoff while leaving regular play unchanged.
