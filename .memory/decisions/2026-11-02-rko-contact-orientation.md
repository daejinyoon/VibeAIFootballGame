# Decisions: RKO Contact Face-Up

- Date: 2026-11-02
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Face-up transition timing
- **Chosen**: Keep the finisher upright during the approach, then turn face-up immediately when the finisher reaches and overlaps the target (choreography time 1.2 seconds).
- **Rationale**: The user clarified that the finisher should not be face-up from the start; the change should occur at target contact.

## D02. Post-contact pose
- **Chosen**: Keep the finisher face-up through the leap, impact, and grounded pose, then preserve the existing recovery blend.
- **Rationale**: This removes the face-down phase while retaining the requested contact timing and existing exit.
