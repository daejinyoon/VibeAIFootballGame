# Decisions: RKO Finisher Lead Spacing

- Date: 2026-11-02
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Forward position after the slam
- **Chosen**: Keep the target at its existing position and move the RKO finisher farther forward so the finisher lands ahead of the target.
- **Rationale**: The user clarified that the performer, not the person being slammed, must be the one protruding forward after the landing.

## D02. Motion timing
- **Chosen**: Ease the finisher's forward glide from its current approach distance to a larger distance during the slam, hold it while grounded, and blend it back during recovery.
- **Rationale**: This preserves the existing contact and face-up landing while making the post-impact ordering clear.
