# Decision: Make Skill Preview Effects Visible

- Direct preview entry must focus the match canvas automatically so the player can trigger the skill with F immediately.
- Tornado Shot needs a large animated funnel around the ball with rotating wind bands and rising particles; the previous small orbit particles were too subtle.
- World Split must visibly split the field surface itself: an unmistakable long, dark trench with the ground plates pushed apart along both sides. At level 1 it should span at least eight prone player lengths, with levels 2 and 3 extending it further.
- The caster must perform a low forward slide along the same line as the opening ground. Keep the gameplay obstacle aligned with that fissure, and use restrained rubble so it supports the split instead of replacing it with a generic wall.
- The split creates a paired-wall barrier: two parallel rock walls rise on either side of the caster's slide lane, leaving the lane between them visible while blocking players and low balls that try to cross either wall.
- Use the supplied fractured-earth reference's dark stone and hot seam palette. The open ground trench and separated turf/earth edges are the primary read.
- Keep a longer cut-in for these large set-piece skills so their activation is easy to notice.
