# Decisions: RKO Landing Spacing

- Date: 2026-11-02
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Landing separation
- **Chosen**: After the RKO impact, move the target farther forward so the target separates from the face-up finisher. Preserve the close kick, contact, and finisher landing pose.
- **Rationale**: The user approved increasing spacing after landing by pushing the target forward, while keeping the existing RKO impact choreography.

## D02. Motion timing
- **Chosen**: Ease the target's forward offset from zero at slam onset to a larger offset by the end of the slam, hold it through the grounded pose, then blend it away during recovery.
- **Rationale**: This makes the spacing change visible after impact without altering the kick or pre-impact setup.
